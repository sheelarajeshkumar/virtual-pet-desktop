# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of positive behavior include showing empathy, respecting differing viewpoints, giving and accepting constructive feedback, taking responsibility, and focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes sexualized language or imagery, trolling, insulting comments, personal or political attacks, public or private harassment, publishing another person's private information without permission, and other conduct reasonably considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Project maintainers clarify and enforce these standards and may remove, edit, or reject contributions or participation that violate them. Moderation decisions should be proportionate, documented privately when practical, and applied consistently.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in project spaces and when an individual officially represents the project in public spaces.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns to the maintainer through [@sheelarajeshkumar](https://github.com/sheelarajeshkumar). If privacy is required, use the repository's [private reporting channel](https://github.com/sheelarajeshkumar/virtual-pet-desktop/security/advisories/new) and prefix the report with `Conduct`.

Maintainers will respect the privacy and security of reporters. A maintainer involved in an incident must not participate in resolving that incident.

## Enforcement guidelines

Maintainers may issue a private correction, warning, temporary restriction, or permanent ban depending on the impact and pattern of behavior.
